# NIPTEI — Launch Strategy (do not forget)

## The important distinction

Do NOT put AliExpress products into the premium custom-formula collection
unless you have independently verified their quality AND have permission
to brand them appropriately.

Keep two collections separate:
- **Premium Custom-Formula Collection** — NIPTEI's own formulas, developed
  with a cosmetic manufacturer / chemist, tested, safety-reviewed.
- **Dropshipping Collection** — AliExpress / supplier products, only after
  samples, quality checks, and approval. Listed separately, never mixed
  into the premium collection.

## Do not launch everything at once

### Phase 1 — Establish the brand
- Finalize NIPTEI logo and visual identity (black, gold, emerald green)
- Choose premium packaging
- Register the business and secure the brand name
- Build the online store

### Phase 2 — Develop original formulas
Start with three hero products only:
1. NIPTEI Nourishing Hair Growth Oil
2. NIPTEI Hydrating Shampoo
3. NIPTEI Restorative Deep Conditioner

Work with a cosmetic manufacturer or chemist to develop and test formulas.

### Phase 3 — Launch dropshipping
- Identify reliable AliExpress hair vendors
- Order samples before listing anything
- Check hair quality, shipping times, and supplier consistency
- Import approved products into your store only

### Phase 4 — Launch and grow
- Introduce both collections (separately labelled)
- Create product photography and videos
- Build social media marketing
- Introduce bundles and complementary offers

## Protect NIPTEI's premium reputation

- Do NOT rely on a supplier saying their products are "FDA approved".
  Cosmetics generally do not receive FDA premarket approval, although
  safety substantiation, labeling, and MoCRA obligations apply.
- Have a qualified professional review formula safety.
- Ensure ingredient lists and labels meet US requirements.
- Avoid unsubstantiated claims that products cure hair loss or treat
  medical conditions. Use: nourish, strengthen, support healthy-looking hair.
- Verify manufacturers' quality controls and insurance.
- Made in USA: only use it when the product genuinely meets FTC
  U.S.-origin requirements, or use "Made in USA with globally sourced
  ingredients" when eligible.
- Reviews: only real customer reviews, with permission. Never invent
  reviews, ratings, or review counts.
